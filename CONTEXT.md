# Glossary

- **Lead mode:** the review skill used by a team lead on a whole deck.
- **Student mode:** a simpler check of a consultant's work against the chapter's principles, not the
  full review.
- **Finding:** one issue the reviewer raises. It always carries a verbatim quote and where it is in
  the deck, so the consultant can check it.
- **Attempt gate:** the reviewer refuses to show any findings until the person has typed their own
  read of the work. Built as a gated and an ungated variant; the board picks which ships.
